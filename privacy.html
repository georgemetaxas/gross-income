Privacy & Terms

Last updated 22 October 2026

Gross Income (grossincome.com.au) shows how much you earn while you're on the toilet. This page explains what we keep and the rules for paying for the Golden Throne. Short version: your salary never leaves your phone.

What stays on your device

  • Your salary and pay period.
  • Your bog sessions, game high scores and settings.

These are saved in your browser's local storage. We never see them unless you sign in to Poop Pool (see below). Clearing your browser data deletes them.

What we keep if you sign in

Signing in is optional. It's needed for Poop Pool (leaderboards with your mates) and the Golden Throne unlock. If you sign in, we store:

  • Your email address, used to send login codes.
  • Your throne name and emoji, shown to people in your pools.
  • Each bog session's start time and length.
  • The dollars earned per session, only if you turn off "Share minutes only". Turning it back on deletes them from our server.
  • The pools you're in, and whether you've unlocked the Golden Throne.

Pool mates only see totals (time, number of bogs, longest bog, and dollars if you share them), never your individual sessions or your salary.

This data is stored with Supabase, our database provider. Login emails are sent through Cloudflare.

Payments

Payments on the website are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card details. Stripe tells us that a payment went through and which Gross Income login it belongs to. In the iPhone and Android apps, payments go through Apple or Google instead.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data.
  • We don't use advertising trackers today. If ads are added later, this page will be updated first, and Golden Throne owners won't see them.

Deleting your data

You can leave a pool or delete your bog sessions in the app at any time. To delete your account and everything stored with it, email hello@grossincome.com.au from the address you signed in with, and we'll delete it within 30 days.

Golden Throne terms

  • The Golden Throne is a one-off payment of A$4.99 (including GST). It unlocks every Bog Arcade game for the Gross Income login you bought it with. It's not a subscription.
  • It works on any device you sign in on with that login. Turd Snake and Poop Pool stay free for everyone.
  • If something's wrong, email us within 30 days and we'll refund you. This doesn't limit your rights under Australian Consumer Law.
  • Games may change, be added or be retired over time.
  • Gross Income is a joke app. The earnings it shows are estimates for fun, not financial or employment advice. Don't use it to argue with your boss.
