Privacy & Terms

Last updated 30 October 2026

Gross Income (grossincome.com.au) shows how much you earn while you're on the toilet. Below is what we collect and the rules for buying the Golden Throne.

The short version

  • We never see your salary. It's saved on your phone and all the maths happens there.
  • You don't need an account. Without one, your bogs never leave your phone.
  • The only things we count for everyone are anonymous game stats: which Bog Arcade game was opened and for how long.
  • If you join Poop Pool, we store your email, throne name and how long your bogs were, plus your dollars if you choose to share them.
  • We don't show ads, use tracking cookies or sell your data.

Your salary

The salary and pay period you type in are saved in your browser's local storage on your device. They're never sent to us or anyone else, and there's no salary field anywhere on our server.

The one way money can leave your phone is if you're in Poop Pool and turn off "Share minutes only". It's on by default. If you turn it off:

  • The dollars you earned on each bog are uploaded with its length.
  • Your pool mates see your total dollars and total time on the leaderboard.
  • Dollars and time together can be used to work out roughly what you earn per hour. Your pool mates can do that maths, and so can anyone with access to our database, so only share dollars with people you'd be happy to know your rough pay.
  • Turn "Share minutes only" back on and the dollar amounts are wiped from our server.

What stays on your device

  • Your salary and pay period.
  • Your Bog Log (every bog's time, length and dollars).
  • Game progress and settings.

Clearing your browser data, or deleting the home screen app, deletes them. We can't recover them for you.

Motion sensor

While a bog is running, the app can use your phone's motion sensor to notice if you've walked off without pressing Finish. iPhones ask your permission first. The readings are checked on your phone and never sent anywhere.

Game stats

When you open a Bog Arcade game, the app sends us the game's name, how long it was open and whether it hit the Golden Throne paywall. There's no account, device ID or score attached, so we can't tell who played. We use it to see which games people like.

If you sign in

Signing in is optional. It's needed for Poop Pool (leaderboards with your mates) and the Golden Throne. If you sign in, we store:

  • Your email address, to send login codes and answer you if you contact us.
  • Your throne name and emoji, shown to people in your pools.
  • Each bog's start time and length, plus dollars only if you share them (see above).
  • The pools you've made or joined.
  • Whether you have the Golden Throne: your plan, when it renews or ends, and Stripe's customer and subscription references.

Pool mates see totals (time, number of bogs, longest bog, and dollars if you share them). They never see your individual bogs, your email or your salary.

Who helps us run it

  • Supabase stores the sign-in data above and the anonymous game stats. It also keeps standard login records, like IP address and time.
  • Cloudflare hosts the website and sends our emails. Like any web host it sees your IP address and browser details when you load the site, and uses them to keep the site running and secure. It also forwards emails you send us to our Gmail inbox (Google).
  • Stripe handles payments on the website. We never see or store your card details. Stripe tells us a payment went through and which Gross Income login it belongs to. In future iPhone and Android apps, payments will go through Apple or Google instead.

Our Supabase database is in Sydney, Australia. Cloudflare, Stripe and Google run worldwide, so what they handle may be processed outside Australia, including in the United States.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your data.
  • We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. If ads are added later, we'll update this page first, and Golden Throne owners won't see them.
  • We don't send marketing emails. The only emails you'll get are login codes.

Seeing, fixing or deleting your data

  • Change your throne name, emoji and dollar sharing in Profile & Crap at any time.
  • Delete bogs from your Bog Log, or leave a pool, in the app.
  • To get a copy of what we hold about you, or to delete your account and everything stored with it, email dropaline@grossincome.com.au from the address you signed in with. We'll do it within 30 days. Payment records Stripe must keep for tax law stay with Stripe.

We keep your sign-in data until you ask us to delete it. Anonymous game stats aren't linked to anyone, so they can't be deleted per person.

Keeping it safe

Data travels over HTTPS. Database rules mean only you can read your own bogs, and pool mates can only read pool totals. If there's ever a breach that's likely to harm you, we'll tell you as soon as we can.

Australian privacy law

Gross Income is made in Melbourne, Australia (ABN 92 638 218 824). We use the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 as our guide for how we handle personal information. As a small business we may not be legally bound by them, but we choose to follow them anyway.

If you have a privacy question or complaint, email us first and we'll reply within 30 days. If you're not happy with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

Changes

If we change what we collect, we'll update this page and the date at the top before the change goes live.

Golden Throne terms

  • Every Bog Arcade game is free for everyone until 4 January 2027.
  • After that, the Golden Throne unlocks every Bog Arcade game for the Gross Income login you sign up with. It's a subscription: A$2.99 a month or A$24.99 a year.
  • It renews automatically at the end of each month or year and charges the card you signed up with, until you cancel. Cancel anytime from Profile & Crap (Manage subscription) or by emailing us. You keep the games until the end of the period you've paid for.
  • If we ever change the price, we'll tell you at least 30 days before your next renewal, so you can cancel first.
  • Founding Flushers (anyone with a login made before 2 November 2026) keep every Bog Arcade game free, forever.
  • It works on any device you sign in on with that login. Turd Snake and Poop Pool stay free for everyone.
  • If something's wrong, email us within 30 days of a charge and we'll refund it. This doesn't limit your rights under Australian Consumer Law.
  • Games may change, be added or be retired over time.
  • Gross Income is a joke app. The earnings it shows are estimates for fun, not financial or employment advice. Don't use it to argue with your boss.
